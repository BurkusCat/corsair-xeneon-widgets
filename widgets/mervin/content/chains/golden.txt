@chain kettle-watching
@topic kitchen
@start idle
- They say a watched kettle never boils.
  | Someone told me a watched kettle never boils.
- So I watched one. For science.
- It boiled. Took about three minutes. Very normal kettle.
- Then I felt bad, because I'd proved everyone wrong, and they seemed so sure.
  | I didn't tell anyone. Didn't want to make a fuss.
= So now I just look slightly to the left of it. Keeps everyone happy.
  | Anyway, I don't watch it any more. Out of respect.
