# Mervin (3D widget)

A real-time three.js scene of a Sims-style studio flat in one-point perspective, with Mervin,
a relaxed blue alien from Planet 6, living in it. He walks about and does about 30
activities, says something every 15 s to 2 min, and when tapped tells a chain of thought one
bubble per tap (Seaman-style; a tap within 30 s continues it). Tapping furniture sends him to
use it. No network, no bridge: everything (mesh, textures, phrases) is generated into the
widget at build time or drawn at runtime.

```
content/*.txt ──► tools/content-build.mjs ──► widget/scripts/content.js   (window.MERVIN_CONTENT)
tools/mesh/*  ──► build-mesh.mjs (SDF ► mesh) ► widget/scripts/mesh.js      (window.MERVIN_MESH)
src/**/*.js   ──► esbuild (iife, chrome130) ──► widget/scripts/mervin.js    (the app + three.js)
```

All three are generated by `build.mjs` and git-ignored. `scripts/widgets.mjs` runs it before
`validate` and `package`. The mesh step is cached by a hash of `tools/mesh/` (about 10 s when
it runs). Content problems fail the build; `node widgets/mervin/build.mjs --lenient` leaves out
broken content files instead (for while writing).

## Mervin's mesh

Sculpted as signed-distance primitives in `tools/mesh/mervin-sdf.mjs` (A-pose, mouth slightly
open, model space in metres, feet at y = 0, facing +z, his left = +x). `build-mesh.mjs`:
narrow-band surface nets at 3 mm, Newton projection onto the surface, meshoptimizer simplify to
32k triangles, tangential relaxation, then per-vertex normals (SDF gradient), region colour and
paints, baked AO, and skin weights (softmax over nearby primitives per bone, split along long
primitives, 10 Laplacian passes, top 4, quantised so each vertex sums to exactly 255; a sum
over 255 makes vertices fly off as spikes). Eyes, lids, teeth, chain links and bracelet are
separate meshes on bones, placed from `anchors`. The chain is projected onto the skin at build time.

Change the sculpt, then `node widgets/mervin/build.mjs` and look with
`npm run shot -- mervin --sizes M --query "view=face"` (or `view=body&yaw=30`).

## Rig and poses

Poses (`src/mervin/poses.js`) are rotations relative to the A-pose. Euler `[x, y, z]` in
radians; for his left side: z negative lowers the arm, x negative swings a limb forward, thigh
x negative lifts the knee, shin x positive bends it, spine/neck/head x positive leans forward.
Elbows and wrists (`forearm*`, `hand*`) take `[flex, twist, side]`; upper arms take an optional
4th value, twist (positive turns the elbow's inside toward the body: needed for hands behind
the back, on the hip, or folded on the belly). Keys ending `*` are mirrored to the right side;
wrap one-sided values in `R({...})` to put them on the right. Poses can be functions of time.
Pose sheets: `npm run shot -- mervin --sizes M --query "view=body" --frames 12 --each "__mervinDebug.poseAt('stand,sit,wave', __frame)"`.

Lying down rotates the root (`brain.lieDown`): `back` = Rx(-90°) (head toward -z), `side` =
Rz(+90°) (head toward -x, facing the viewer). Layouts are in `activities.js` (`LIE`).

## Brain

- Activities are generator programs: `yield` waits a frame and returns dt; helpers
  (`walkTo`, `sitOn`, `lieDown`, `getUp`, `wait`, `turnTo`, `moveStraight`, `tweenRoot`) are
  generators used with `yield*`. `walkTo(spot)` paths to the spot's `approach` then walks
  straight to `at` (seats stop at `approach` and back onto the seat). Weights pick the next
  activity from time of day, energy and recency; 23:00 to 07:00 he sleeps in bed.
- Chains pause the program (attention: he stops, turns to the camera, the camera eases into a
  close-up) and resume it after the ending, or after 30 s without a tap (the chain is
  suspended; within 2 h a later tap may resume it with a "resume" line). Chains whose first step
  has `<act:id>` start that activity and keep talking while he does it.
- Taps: his projected bone bounds padded to at least 60 px (or the bubble) > furniture boxes >
  floor (he glances). While typing, a tap finishes the bubble; after that it advances.
- Memory (localStorage key `mervin:<uniqueId>`): first seen, taps, chains heard, last 600 line
  ids, the suspended chain.

## Camera

`core/camera-math.js` has a table per layout (xl, l, m, s, tall-*): horizontal FOV, vanishing
point height, camera height, and whether the camera holds the whole room (XL/L) or follows him
at a distance that fills a fraction of the height (M, S, portrait). The camera never rotates;
it trucks along x and dollies along z (critically damped spring), with a dead zone. Modes:
`normal`, `chat` (closer), `freeze` (held while he walks up to the lens to peek).
`test/camera.test.mjs` checks his head and feet stay in frame everywhere for every size.

## Content

See `content/FORMAT.md` and `content/BIBLE.md` (it ends with the canon the writers settled).
About 2,800 one-liners (a quarter with slots), 130 chains, 70 word lists. `node widgets/mervin/tools/content-lint.mjs [file.txt]` checks
format, slots, tags, tokens, lengths (6 to 140 characters for any expansion), duplicates and
near-duplicates, British spelling and style. Context tags: time of day, weekday, weekend,
`act:<id>`, `rain`, `firstDay`, `longTime`, `furniture:<id>`.

## Debugging and verifying

Query parameters (preview or `file://`): `debug=1` (fps, activity, chain, hit box),
`now=21:30`, `speed=20` (clock), `sim=4` (his behaviour runs 4x), `seed=3`, `activity=dance`,
`rain=1|0`, `lights=sun`, `view=face|body`, `pose=sit`, `poses=a,b&every=1.5`,
`cam=x,y,z,vfov`, and any setting (`quality=low&chattiness=0`). `window.__mervinDebug` has
`pose()`, `activity()`, `tap()` and `brain` for the shot tool's `--eval`/`--each`.

```bash
npm test                                   # includes widgets/mervin/test
npm run validate -- mervin
npm run preview                            # http://127.0.0.1:47900/?widget=mervin
npm run shot -- mervin --portrait          # all 8 sizes from file://, fails on console errors
npm run shot -- mervin --sizes XL --query "now=15:00&debug=1" --eval "__mervinDebug.tap()" --frames 4 --every 1500
```

Headless Edge here uses the real GPU (ANGLE D3D11); `--swiftshader` forces software WebGL.

## Gotchas

- In iCUE 5.52 the EDGE gives this widget `iCUE.fpsLimit` = 10 (widgets are drawn by a separate
  `QmlRenderer.exe` that ticks them), but the limit is advisory: rendering at 30 fps showed up
  as visibly smoother motion on the EDGE (confirmed on the device, 2026-10-15). The "Smooth
  motion (30 fps)" setting (`ignoreFpsLimit`, default on) renders up to 30; off honours the limit.
  10 s after start, and after that switch changes, the widget logs
  `[mervin] running: <renderer>; <fps> fps (iCUE limit N)` as a warning: iCUE's log only keeps
  warnings and errors from widgets, never `console.log`.

- Lighting applies the hour only when it changes; `state.hour` starts at -1 so the first frame
  applies it (otherwise a default overhead light floods the room).
- three r186 removed `PCFSoftShadowMap`; use `PCFShadowMap` with `shadow.radius`.
- Walls and ceiling use `shadowSide: DoubleSide` and cast shadows, and the sun's shadow camera
  is fitted around the whole room box, so sunlight only gets in through the window.
- Widget folder names must sort after `index.html` (only `resources/`, `scripts/`, `styles/`).
- Keep `<head>` free of inline scripts except the groups JSON (iCUE's XML parser).
- The `[hidden]` attribute is overridden by `display: flex`; `widget.css` forces it.
